# Getting help

- **Docs:** [docs.tablepro.app](https://docs.tablepro.app), and [Troubleshooting](https://docs.tablepro.app/troubleshooting) for common problems.
- **Questions:** [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/TableProApp/TablePro/discussions) or [Discord](https://discord.gg/hCNmUUbnD4).
- **Bugs and feature requests** for the app or a plugin: [TablePro issues](https://github.com/TableProApp/TablePro/issues).
- **License, billing or account:** email hello@tablepro.app.
- **A third-party integration:** contact its publisher. Its entry in the [integrations directory](https://github.com/TableProApp/integrations) links to their issues or support page.
- **A security problem:** follow [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/TableProApp/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md), never a public issue.

Replies can take a few days.
